# Smart Study Planner - Project Statement

## 1. Problem Statement

Students often have multiple subjects with different examination dates, difficulty levels, and preparation progress. Manually deciding which subject to study and how much time to allocate can become difficult, especially when examinations are approaching. Therefore, a system is required to organize subject information, determine study priorities, and generate a study plan based on the student's available study time.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on developing a Python-based command-line study planning system. It allows students to manage their profile and subjects, record examination dates, difficulty levels and preparation progress, calculate subject priorities, generate a study plan, identify at-risk subjects, and maintain a history of study sessions. The system uses JSON for local data storage.

## 3. Target Users

* School students
* College students
* Students preparing for multiple examinations
* Learners who want to track their study progress and study time

## 4. High-Level Features

* Student profile management
* Subject management
* Subject priority calculation
* Dynamic study-plan generation
* At-risk subject detection
* Progress tracking
* Study-session logging
* Study-history management
* JSON-based data storage
